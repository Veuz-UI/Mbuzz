Overview

Reliable Indoor Coverage. Seamless
Signal Performance.

Delivering cost-effective and high-quality indoor mobile signal coverage using innovative Distributed Antenna Systems (DAS),
tailored for commercial, public, and high-density environments.

Why IBS?

No More Dead Zones

Eliminate dead zones in buildings with poor reception.

Offload traffic from outdoor cells and meet indoor capacity requirements.

Enhance service quality and reduce interference for consistent performance.
Solutions

How We Achieve IBS

We engineer the right-fit system based on building type, traffic needs, and RF environment:

Passive DAS

Ideal for compact buildings with high reliability.

Leaky Cable

Perfect for tunnels or underground railway systems.

Active DAS (Fiber)

Best for large venues needing flexible, high-capacity systems.

Hybrid Active Solutions

Combining the best of passive and active designs.
