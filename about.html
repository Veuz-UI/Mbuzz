Who We Are

MBUZZ is a leading Saudi ICT solutions provider and technology enabler, shaping the future through artificial intelligence and advanced digital solutions.

Founded in 2007 and transformed into a joint-stock company in 2022, MBUZZ has been a trusted partner to enterprises, governments, and telecom providers, delivering AI-driven innovation alongside secure, scalable infrastructure across connectivity, cloud, cybersecurity, and IT systems integration. Headquartered in Riyadh, with operations across the Middle East and regional offices worldwide, MBUZZ blends local expertise with global reach to accelerate digital transformation across sectors.

Strength in Numbers

With partnerships spanning over 50 global brands in more than 40 countries, MBUZZ is a trusted strategic partner in driving innovation and infrastructure development. Backed by a team of 500+ experts, we are committed to delivering high-impact, sustainable value to our clients across the region.
