Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect and use information about you when you use our website www.mbuzz.com (and any components thereof, the "Site") or otherwise interact with us through it.

We are Mobile Phone Business Co., a closed joint stock company established in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia ("KSA"), national unified establishment number (7018061007), with our office located at Building CS-01, Al-Raidah Digital City, Riyadh, KSA, referred to as "MBUZZ" (the "Company", "we", "us"), and we are the data controller for the processing of your personal data under the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law issued by Royal Decree No. (M/19) dated 09/02/1443H, as amended, and its Implementing Regulations (the "PDPL").

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By using the Site or submitting your data through it, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Policy. If you do not agree to it, please do not use the Site or submit your data through it.

The Site is not intended for individuals under the age of 18, and we do not knowingly collect data relating to them.

1. Changes to This Policy

We may modify this Policy from time to time, in which case we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. If we make material changes to the way we process your data, we will use reasonable efforts to notify you by appropriate means consistent with the PDPL. Your continued use of the Site after publication of an update constitutes acknowledgment of it.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide to Us

You may provide some or all of the following information when using the Site’s features:

  • Basic contact details: first name, last name, email address, mobile number, and the content of your message, when using the "Contact Us" form.
  • Job application data: name, email address, mobile number, location, current/previous job title, message content, and the uploaded CV, including any additional data you choose to include within it.
  • Newsletter subscription data: email address.

You may elect not to provide this information, but doing so may prevent you from using the related feature. We do not collect sensitive personal data through the Site, and we ask job applicants not to include in their CVs any data unnecessary for evaluating their application.

2.2 Information We Collect Automatically

When you visit the Site, limited technical data is collected automatically, including your IP address, browser type, operating system, server logs, and the pages you access, for the purposes of operating, securing, and improving the Site.

3. How We Use Your Information

3.1 Processing based on taking steps at your request:

  • Responding to your inquiries submitted via the contact form.
  • Evaluating job applications and communicating with you regarding them in anticipation of a potential contract.

3.2 Processing based on your explicit consent:

  • Sending you our newsletter and marketing materials where you have subscribed.

3.3 Processing based on our legitimate interests:

  • Administering and operating the Site, conducting maintenance, and analyzing performance.
  • Maintaining the security of the Site, our systems, and our users, and preventing misuse.

3.4 In addition, we may use your information to comply with our legal obligations, enforce our policies, and protect or defend our rights and the rights of our users.

We commit that contact form data will not be used for any marketing purposes.

4. Consent, Its Records, and Withdrawal

When you submit your data through the Site’s forms, we record your consent, including its timestamp, scope, and the version of this Policy you agreed to. You may withdraw your consent at any time — without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal — via the unsubscribe link available in every marketing email, or by contacting us as set out in Section (12). Withdrawal of consent will not deprive you of any service that does not require such consent.

5. Disclosure of Your Information

We do not sell or trade your personal data. Disclosure is limited to the following circumstances:

  • Service providers processing data on our behalf, under our instructions, and to the extent necessary to provide their services: cloud hosting services (Amazon Web Services — AWS); the email marketing platform (Zoho), limited to newsletter subscribers’ data; content delivery and security services (Cloudflare); and frontend library delivery services (jsDelivr).
  • Regulators, judicial authorities, or law enforcement agencies, where disclosure is required by law or ordered by a competent authority, or to protect our rights, the safety of our users, or to exercise or defend legal claims.
  • Professional advisors (such as auditors or law firms) within our legitimate interests and to the extent necessary.

CVs are not shared with any party outside the Company.

6. Where We Store Your Information; Cross-Border Transfer

The Site and its databases are hosted with AWS in the Mumbai region, Republic of India; accordingly, your data is stored and processed outside the KSA. This transfer is carried out in accordance with Article (29) of the PDPL and the Regulation on Personal Data Transfer Outside the Kingdom, and we take the steps required by law to ensure your data is treated securely and with a level of protection no less than that prescribed by the PDPL, through legally recognized appropriate safeguards.

7. Retention of Your Information

We retain your data for as long as necessary to fulfill the purpose of its collection or to comply with legal requirements, as follows:

Data Category Retention Period
Contact form submissions Twelve (12) months from receipt, then automatically deleted
Unsuccessful CVs and applications Twelve (12) months from submission, then automatically deleted, unless you consent to retention for future opportunities
Newsletter subscription data Until consent is withdrawn, with a withdrawal record retained as evidence of compliance

Data is securely and irreversibly destroyed once the purpose of its collection has been fulfilled.

8. Your Rights

The PDPL grants you the following rights, subject to any limitations under applicable law:

  • Right to Be Informed: to be informed of how we collect, process, store, and destroy your data and the legal basis for doing so, as set out in this Policy.
  • Right of Access: to request access to your personal data and obtain a copy of it in a clear and readable format.
  • Right to Request Correction: to request the correction, completion, or updating of your data where inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Right to Request Destruction: to request the destruction of your data in the circumstances permitted by law, subject to any retention obligations.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: to withdraw your consent to processing at any time, as set out in Section (4).
  • Objection to Marketing: to opt out of marketing at any time via the unsubscribe option in every message.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us as set out in Section (12); we will respond within the statutory timeframes. You may also submit a complaint to us directly, or to the competent authority — the Saudi Data and AI Authority (SDAIA), contactable via www.sdaia.gov.sa.

9. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

9.1 What Cookies Are

A "cookie" is a small text file stored on your browser or device when you visit a website, helping the website recognize your device and remember your preferences on future visits. Similar technologies operating in a comparable manner may also be used.

9.2 Types by Source and Duration

  • First-party cookies: placed directly by our Site for its operation.
  • Third-party cookies: placed by external parties we work with through our Site.
  • Session cookies: temporary, deleted automatically when you close your browser.
  • Persistent cookies: remain on your device until their expiry date or manual deletion.

9.3 Categories and Purposes

  • Strictly necessary/technical cookies: essential for the Site’s basic operation and security; they do not require your consent and cannot be disabled.
  • Analytical/performance cookies: to understand how the Site is used and improve its performance.
  • Functionality cookies: to remember your preferences, such as language.
  • Marketing/advertising cookies: for marketing purposes, where used.

9.5 How to Control or Decline Cookies

You can control cookies through the consent banner displayed when visiting the Site (Accept / Reject / Customize), or through your browser settings by rejecting cookies or deleting stored ones. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may limit your ability to use certain Site features.

9.6 Tracking Technologies in Our Communications

Our marketing emails may include measurement technologies indicating whether you opened a message or clicked its links, for the purpose of improving the effectiveness of our communications. You can prevent this by configuring your email program to disable the loading of remote images, or by unsubscribing entirely.

10. Third-Party Websites and Links

The Site may contain links to external websites or platforms operated by other parties, each governed by its own independent privacy policies and terms. We are not responsible for the privacy, security, or content of such websites, and the inclusion of a link does not constitute an endorsement of their content. We advise you to review their policies before submitting your data to them. The Site also contains a redirect link to the MBUZZ Technologies website, which operates within the Company’s ecosystem; data processing through it is governed by the privacy policy and terms published on it. The Site further embeds Google Maps to display our office locations, governed by its provider’s privacy policy.

11. Data Security

We apply appropriate organizational, administrative, and technical measures to protect your data against loss, unauthorized access, disclosure, or alteration, proportionate to the nature of the data and the risks of its processing. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we recommend that you do not use unsecure channels to send us sensitive information.

12. How to Contact Us

For any inquiry regarding this Policy or to exercise your rights as a data subject, contact us at the dedicated mailbox: privacy@mbuzz.com

13. Language

This Policy is issued in Arabic and English. In the event of any conflict or inconsistency between the two versions, the Arabic text shall prevail to the extent of the inconsistency.
