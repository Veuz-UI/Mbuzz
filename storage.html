Overview

Data at the Speed of AI. Storage Built for Scale.

Modern AI, HPC, and digital enterprises demand storage that can keep pace with massive data growth, extreme performance requirements, and evolving workloads. MBUZZ delivers end-to-end High-Performance Storage (HPS) and Enterprise NAS solutions that remove data bottlenecks and unlock the full potential of your compute infrastructure.

By combining deep solutioning expertise with the world’s leading storage platforms, we design, deploy, and optimize storage environments that go beyond traditional data repositories. They become engines for AI innovation, research acceleration, and business growth. From ultra-high-throughput parallel file systems to resilient enterprise NAS, MBUZZ provides a scalable foundation for data-driven success.

1. High-Performance Storage (HPS)

Purpose-built for AI training, inference, HPC simulations, and data-intensive research, our High-Performance Storage solutions deliver exceptional throughput, low latency, and linear scalability.

DDN (DataDirect Networks)

Industry-leading performance for the most demanding AI, deep learning, and HPC workloads, trusted by global supercomputing centers and AI innovators.

Weka

A software-defined, flash-native parallel file system designed for extreme performance, cloud-like flexibility, and seamless scaling across hybrid environments.

VAST Data

A revolutionary all-flash data platform that combines high performance with the economics of archive storage, simplifying AI data pipelines at scale.

Services

Storage Consulting & Implementation

Beyond technology, MBUZZ provides end-to-end storage consulting and delivery services to ensure optimal outcomes:

Infrastructure Sizing & Architecture Design

Workload-driven sizing aligned with AI models, throughput requirements, and future growth, avoiding over-provisioning and reducing total cost of ownership (TCO).

Deployment & Integration

End-to-end implementation by certified engineers, including seamless integration with GPU clusters, AI platforms, and existing data center environments.

Performance Optimization

Advanced tuning of data paths, file systems, and storage orchestration to achieve maximum throughput and minimal latency for mission-critical workloads.

Sovereign & Compliant Data Solutions

Deep expertise in regional data residency, compliance, and security requirements to keep sensitive data local and protected.
