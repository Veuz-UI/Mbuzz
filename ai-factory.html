Accelerated by NVIDIA: Enterprise AI at Scale

Transform AI Data Centers into Intelligent AI Factories

NVIDIA accelerated computing platforms unify GPUs, CPUs, networking, and enterprise software into a powerful foundation for AI innovation. With NVIDIA AI Enterprise and NVIDIA Mission Control with Run: ai orchestration, organizations gain the agility, resiliency, and efficiency required to scale AI into production—performing exponentially more work in less time with lower energy consumption.

AI Factory is a purpose-built computing platform designed to transform raw data into intelligence — measured by token throughput — powering decisions, automation, and enterprise AI solutions. Built on NVIDIA accelerated computing platforms and orchestrated by Mission Control with Run: ai, AI Factories simplify hardware, networking, and enterprise-grade software into a scalable, resilient, and energy-efficient foundation for AI.
