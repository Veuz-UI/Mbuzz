Overview

Wireless Networks That Go Further

Today’s networks need to be fast, reliable, and ready to scale. As demand for high-bandwidth applications continues to grow, traditional wired connectivity isn’t always practical or available. Meeting these demands is where MBUZZ delivers.

MBUZZ is a trusted wireless systems integrator and value-added reseller, delivering carrier-grade wireless solutions across the The Globe. We’ve successfully deployed and operated thousands of microwave network nodes for government entities, telecom operators, and enterprise customers, connecting cities, remote sites, and critical infrastructure.

Our Approach

Purpose-Built for Your Network

MBUZZ works as an independent integrator, selecting the right technologies, vendors, and designs to match your exact requirements. From the initial field survey to final optimization, we design and deliver wireless networks that are reliable, secure, and cost-effective.

Whether your challenge is distance, terrain, speed, or time-to-deploy, we have the experience to solve it.

Solutions

Built to Connect. Designed to Scale

Point-to-Point (PTP) Microwave

PTP microwave is a proven alternative to fiber, delivering high-capacity, secure links over long distances. It’s ideal for connecting remote sites, backbone links, and mission-critical networks, without the cost and delay of fiber rollout.

Point-to-Multipoint (PMP) Microwave

For high-density areas, PMP microwaves allow multiple sites to connect through a single hub. It’s efficient, scalable, and perfect for enterprise access, city-wide coverage, and service provider networks.

Carrier-Grade
Wi-Fi

We deliver indoor and outdoor Wi-Fi solutions that provide seamless user experience and centralized control. From public hotspots to enterprise campuses, our Wi-Fi networks are secure, easy to manage, and built for scale.

IoT & Industrial IoT
Connectivity

MBUZZ provides reliable wireless connectivity for IoT and Industrial IoT. From smart metering to offshore platforms, we connect field devices to operation centers using secure, high availability networks in any environment.
